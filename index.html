About Us

The Mozilla Open Source Community at SSGMCE is a vibrant student-led community dedicated to fostering innovation through collaborative open-source development.

Located in the Department of Information Technology, we bridge the gap between academic theory and industry practice by working on real-world projects and participating in global open-source initiatives.

Community first

Building a supportive environment where every developer can thrive and share knowledge.

Open Source

Contributing to the global ecosystem by building software that remains open and accessible to all.
